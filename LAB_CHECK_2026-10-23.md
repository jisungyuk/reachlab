## 2. Sampling rate (main fix)

Samples are now recorded once per Liberty frame on the reader thread, not on
the screen timer (old data: ~107 Hz, 6–16 ms gaps, ~0.5% of rows repeating the
previous row's coordinates exactly).

```bash
python scripts/check_trial_files.py <data folder>/<participant>/session_NNN_YYYYMMDD
```

- [ ] Recorded rate (`Hz` column) ≈ Liberty read rate from step 1.
- [ ] `dt` min/max close to the median (no 16 ms gaps).
- [ ] `repeated rows` ≈ 0% (an exact repeat of every coordinate means no new
      Liberty data arrived for that sample).
